Starting a blog has always been in the back of my head, but I've never gotten to actually doing it seriously. The most I've ever done was make a Bear blog, ([which you can still access here!](https://pxlinspace.bearblog.dev/)) and then I never touched it ever again. 


Now it's time to start for real! This time, I'm making my own site using the [Fuwari template](https://github.com/saicaca/fuwari). As much as I love Bear's philosophy, having my own site just felt more fun. Also, I don't feel like paying $5 a month for images on blog posts.

# Why start a blog?

1. I want to be a part of the slower old internet: a space that is not dictated by companies, algorithms, or attention.
2. I want to have a centralized site to hold all of my miscellaneous long-form writings so they can be conveniently shared around!
3. I honestly have a really bad writing stamina and I lowk need practice XD

I'm aiming to primarily use social media for publicizing my projects (and shitposting). This blog and my website are my spaces for yapping.

# ...But what will I blog?

I kind of want to say that this blog could just be a place where I talk about literally anything, but I should probably give this blog some sense of direction. 

Right now, my interests are in game development and design, visual art and animation, cool software tools, and anything fun and creative really! Since I'm only getting started with this blog, I'll do my best to start by posting at least once every two months.


# How can I follow this blog?

If you're a cool and epic and amazing person, you can follow this blog through its RSS feed ([here's the link](https://blog.pxlin.space/rss.xml)). If you have no idea what an RSS is, [here's a guide by Nicky Case](https://ncase.me/rss/)! My personal favorite RSS reader is Miniflux because of how minimalistic it is.

Otherwise if you're a slightly less cool and epic and amazing person, I'll probably post a link to each blog post on my social accounts, especially my [bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/pxlin.space)!

That's all I have to say for now. I have a few ideas for what I'll blog about next, so stay tuned!
